Customer service

Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver across the United Kingdom. If you're outside the UK, message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll let you know if we can help.

How your order is sent

Every order is sent with a tracked and signed-for service, and high-value items are fully insured while they're with the courier. We'll send you the tracking number on WhatsApp or by email as soon as your order is on its way.

Delivery times

  • Items in stock: usually sent within 1–2 working days of payment, then delivered in 1–3 working days.
  • Sourced items: timings depend on the piece you've asked for. We'll give you an estimate before you pay and keep you updated until it arrives.

Working days are Monday to Friday, not including UK bank holidays. Delivery times are estimates, and occasionally couriers have delays outside our control.

Delivery costs

The delivery cost is confirmed with you when you order, before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Receiving your order

Because orders are signed for, someone will need to be at the delivery address. If you miss the delivery, the courier will leave a card telling you how to rearrange it or collect it. Please check your parcel when it arrives and tell us within 48 hours if anything is damaged or missing.

Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel is lost or arrives damaged, contact us straight away with photos of the packaging and the item. We'll deal with the courier for you and arrange a replacement or a full refund.

Questions

Message us on WhatsApp or email JP.ReserveCo@gmail.com.
