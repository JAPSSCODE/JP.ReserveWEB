Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 18 October 2026

We deliver throughout the United Kingdom. If you're based outside the UK, please contact us on WhatsApp before placing your order and we'll be happy to help.

Every order is sent by Tracked 24 (T24), so you can follow your purchase every step of the way, and high-value items are fully insured while they're with the courier. As soon as your order is on its way, we'll send you a tracking number on WhatsApp or by email.

Orders should arrive within 1–3 days of your order date. We always aim for next-day delivery, but please allow up to 3 days for your order to reach you. The delivery cost is confirmed with you when you order, before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Orders are signed for, so someone will need to be at the delivery address to receive yours. If you miss the delivery, the courier will leave a card explaining how to rearrange delivery or collect your parcel. Please check your parcel when it arrives and let us know within 48 hours if anything is damaged or missing.

JP.ReserveCo is not responsible for parcels that go missing because an incorrect delivery address was provided, or for parcels that aren't collected from the courier after a failed delivery attempt. JP.ReserveCo is also not responsible for any dispatch delays caused by our courier, and refunds or exchanges will not be given on this basis.

This doesn't affect your statutory rights. If your parcel is lost in transit or arrives damaged, contact us straight away with photos of the packaging and the item, and we'll resolve it with the courier for you.

For any questions, message us on WhatsApp or email JP.ReserveCo@gmail.com.
