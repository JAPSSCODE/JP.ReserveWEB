Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Your right to cancel

Because you buy from us without visiting a shop, you have the right to cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of receiving it. This applies to new and pre-loved items, including items we've sourced for you.

How to return an item

  • Tell us you'd like to return it within 14 days of delivery, on WhatsApp or by email.
  • Send it back within 14 days of telling us, using a tracked and insured service.
  • Please return it in the same condition you received it, with all tags, packaging, dust bags, boxes, receipts and accessories.

Unless the item is faulty or not as described, you pay the cost of sending it back.

Condition of returns

You're welcome to inspect an item the way you would in a shop. If it comes back worn, damaged, or missing its tags or packaging, we may reduce your refund to reflect the loss in value. Every return is checked against the photos and authentication records we took before it was sent.

Refunds

We'll refund you within 14 days of receiving the item back (or of you showing us proof you've returned it, if that's sooner). Refunds go back using the method you paid with and include the standard delivery cost you paid.

Faulty items or items not as described

If something isn't right, contact us as soon as possible with photos. We'll cover the return postage and offer a repair, a replacement or a full refund. This doesn't affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Authenticity

Everything we sell is checked before it's sent. If an independent authentication service ever finds that an item from us isn't genuine, we'll give you a full refund, including all postage costs.

Contact

Message us on WhatsApp or email JP.ReserveCo@gmail.com. We'll tell you where to send your return.
