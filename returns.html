Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Standard returns

If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you have 14 days from the date of delivery to return it for a refund (minus shipping costs), an exchange or a credit note.

Refunds are processed within 2 working days of us receiving and accepting your return. Exchanges and credit notes are processed the same working day. Once a refund has been sent, the time it takes to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider, so please contact them if you have any questions about timing.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene reasons, swimwear, underwear and socks can't be returned. Items sold at a discount because of a fault, and items sourced specifically for you by our personal shopping service, are also non-returnable. Not sure about sizing? Message us before you buy and we'll send you detailed measurements, so you can choose the best fit.

Goyard: all Goyard pieces are sourced individually for each client, so every Goyard sale is final. We're unable to offer returns, refunds or exchanges on Goyard items.

Condition of returns

To be accepted, your item must be unused and in exactly the condition you received it, in its original packaging, with all tags still attached. Security tags must not be removed, and we can't accept items where tags have been taken off and put back on. Please include any box, dust bag, receipt and accessories that came with it.

Every item is quality-checked before it leaves us, and every return is carefully inspected when it arrives. If an item has been worn, washed, damaged or had its tags removed, we reserve the right to refuse the return, and the item will be sent back to you at your cost.

How to return an item

Contact us on WhatsApp or email JP.ReserveCo@gmail.com and we'll send you everything you need to complete your return. Your item must be sent back within 14 days of delivery using Royal Mail, otherwise it can't be accepted. Return postage is your responsibility.

Returns checklist

  • Return within 14 days of delivery
  • Return postage is paid by the buyer
  • Items must be unused, with all tags and packaging
  • Items must be sent back by Royal Mail

Sale items

Sale items have exactly the same return rights as full-price items.

Faulty or damaged items

Every piece is checked in detail before dispatch. If your item arrives with a fault or damage, contact us with your order details and clear photos of the issue, and we'll review whether it qualifies for a full refund. We can't accept returns for items that have been washed, altered or damaged through wear and tear.

Sending your return

We strongly recommend using Royal Mail's tracked and insured service, as we can't process a return that doesn't reach us, whether it's lost in the post or sent to the wrong address. Items remain the property of JP.ReserveCo until they've been delivered to you and payment has been received in full. Once a return has been received and accepted by us, ownership of the item passes back to JP.ReserveCo.

This policy doesn't affect your statutory rights.
