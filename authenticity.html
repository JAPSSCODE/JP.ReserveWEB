About us

Authenticity Guarantee

100% authentic, every time

At JP.ReserveCo, our aim is simple: to be the name you trust most for authentic luxury.

We've built a network of reputable suppliers and verified boutiques, many of whom buy directly from the brands you see on our site. Combined with our in-depth knowledge of the luxury market, this means we know exactly where every piece comes from before it ever reaches you.

Every item, whether brand new or pre-loved, is carefully inspected before it's offered for sale. We check the materials, stitching, hardware, labels, serial numbers and packaging against the brand's own standards. If there's ever any doubt, we simply don't sell it.

Many brands, including Moncler and Stone Island, can be verified through their unique authenticity tags, codes and serial numbers, giving you complete peace of mind that your piece is genuine.

We take a firm stand against counterfeit goods. We never knowingly buy or sell replicas, and we strongly oppose anyone who supplies fake products to consumers. Our reputation is built on genuine pieces and an exceptional standard of service.

We stand behind every sale. If a recognised independent authentication service ever confirms that an item you bought from us is not genuine, we'll give you a full refund, including delivery costs. Full details can be found in our Terms & Conditions.

For extra reassurance on any piece, whether you'd like more photos, a closer look at a serial number or simply a conversation before you buy, message us on WhatsApp or email Jake@jpreserveco.info.

JP.ReserveCo is an independent reseller and isn't affiliated with, authorised or endorsed by any of the brands we sell. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
