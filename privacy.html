Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

JP.ReserveCo is responsible for the personal information described here. If you have any questions about your data, email JP.ReserveCo@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your first name and email address, and a record that you agreed to receive marketing emails. We use these to send you offers, new arrivals and news. Our legal basis is your consent.
  • Orders, sourcing and selling enquiries: the details you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram or email (such as your name, the item you want or are selling, photos, delivery address and phone number). We use these to answer you, complete your order and deliver it. Our legal basis is to take steps towards, and carry out, a contract with you.
  • Records we must keep: sales records are kept as long as UK tax law requires.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it with services that help us run the business:

  • Brevo, which stores our mailing list and sends our emails;
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you choose to message us there;
  • delivery couriers, who need your name and address to deliver your order.

Some of these services may process data outside the UK, protected by the safeguards UK law requires.

How long we keep it

Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Order and enquiry details are kept for as long as we need them to look after your order and meet our legal obligations, then deleted.

Unsubscribing

Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email us and we'll remove you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email us to make a request. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and browser storage

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It saves a few small settings in your own browser so the site works the way you'd expect:

  • the items you save to your wishlist;
  • whether you've signed up to, or closed, the newsletter pop-up, so it doesn't keep appearing.

These stay on your device and aren't sent to us. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
