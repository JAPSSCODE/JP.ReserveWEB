Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we keep it safe and the choices you have. It applies to our website, our WhatsApp and Instagram conversations, our emails and our newsletter.

At a glance

  • We only collect what we need to serve you
  • We never sell your personal information
  • Marketing emails are only sent with your permission
  • No advertising or tracking cookies on our website
  • You can ask to see or delete your data at any time

1. Who we are

JP.ReserveCo is a UK-based luxury personal shopping, sourcing and resale business. We are the "controller" of your personal information, which means we decide how it's used and are responsible for looking after it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

For any privacy question or request, email JP.ReserveCo@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.

2. The information we collect

When you enquire or order: your name, phone number, email address, Instagram username, delivery address, the items you're interested in, your order history and our messages with you.

When you request sourcing: the item, size, budget and any preferences you share with us.

When you sell an item to us: your name and contact details, photos and details of the item, and the bank details we need to pay you.

When you join our newsletter: your first name, email address and a record of when you agreed to receive marketing emails. Our email provider also records whether our emails are opened and which links are clicked, which helps us send emails you'll find useful.

When you leave a review: your first name, your review and any photo or video you choose to send us.

We don't collect payment card details. Payments are made by bank transfer, so we only see the name and reference on payments you send us.

The law requires us to have a valid reason, called a "lawful basis", for each way we use your information:

4. Marketing

We'll only send you marketing emails if you've ticked the consent box when signing up. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email, or by contacting us. Unsubscribing won't affect any order or welcome discount you've already received.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with trusted services that help us run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • Brevo: stores our mailing list and sends our emails
  • Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram): when you choose to message us there
  • Google: our email account and the fonts used on our website
  • Royal Mail and other couriers: your name and address, to deliver your order
  • Our bank: to send and receive payments
  • Our website host: to keep this website online
  • Professional advisers: such as an accountant, where needed

We may also share information where the law requires it, for example with HMRC, the police or a court, or to protect our legal rights.

6. International transfers

Some of these services store data outside the UK, for example in the European Union or the United States. Where this happens, the transfer is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved data transfer agreements.

When you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a "do not contact" list so we don't email you again by mistake.

8. Keeping it safe

Customer details are kept in our own private customer records system, which only we can access. We use strong passwords and keep our devices secure. No system can be completely secure, but if a breach ever puts your information at risk, we'll tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires us to.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the information we hold about you
  • have inaccurate information corrected
  • have your information deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive your information in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time

To use any of these rights, email us. It's free, and we'll respond within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first. Some rights have exceptions, for example we must keep sales records for tax purposes even if you ask us to delete them.

10. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small files that websites save on your device. Our website does not use any advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so we don't show a cookie banner.

To make the site work the way you'd expect, it saves a few small settings in your own browser's storage:

NameWhat it doesHow long
jp-wishlistRemembers the items you've saved with the heart buttonUntil you clear it
jp-recentRemembers the last pieces you looked at, for the "Recently viewed" rowUntil you clear it
jp-newsletterRemembers if you've signed up or closed the newsletter pop-up, so it doesn't keep appearingUntil you clear it

These settings stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google and shares your IP address so the fonts can be delivered. When you click a WhatsApp, Instagram or email link, you leave our website, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

11. Children

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we use your information, please contact us first and we'll do our best to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
